# Awesome-Banking-Collections-Management

# Top Collections Management (Banking) Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Debt Recovery, Collections Automation & Loan Management*  
**Last updated: March 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Collections Management in Banking**. These tools manage delinquent accounts, automate collection workflows, predict borrower behavior, and optimize recovery strategies for banks, fintech lenders, debt collection agencies, and microfinance institutions.

**Examples** include Qualco, TrueAccord, CollectAI, Finvi, CGI Collections, FICO Debt Manager, Katabat, Indebted, Pair Finance, and Receeve (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is expanded with active projects for self-hosting, custom collection workflows, and transparent debt management — ideal for fintech startups, microfinance institutions, and developers building vendor-independent collections solutions. Note that the open-source ecosystem for full-scale enterprise collections management remains limited, with most projects focused on loan management systems (LMS), AI-powered collection agents, or research-grade predictive models.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Qualco](https://www.qualco.eu/)**  
  End-to-end collections and debt management platform covering early-stage to late-stage recovery with analytics and workflow automation.

- **[TrueAccord](https://www.trueaccord.com/)**  
  AI-powered digital collections platform using machine learning to personalize recovery journeys and improve liquidation rates.

- **[CollectAI](https://www.collect.ai/)**  
  AI-driven receivables management platform automating collections communications across email, SMS, and voice channels.

- **[Finvi](https://finvi.com/)**  
  Enterprise collections and recovery software with workflow automation, compliance tools, and payment processing for creditors and agencies.

- **[CGI Collections](https://www.cgi.com/)**  
  Comprehensive debt collection and recovery solution covering case management, payment processing, and regulatory compliance.

- **[FICO Debt Manager](https://www.fico.com/)**  
  Enterprise collections and recovery platform with predictive analytics, treatment optimization, and comprehensive case management.

- **[Katabat](https://katabat.com/)**  
  Collections and recovery platform with digital engagement, treatment optimization, and compliance management capabilities.

- **[Indebted](https://indebted.co/)**  
  AI-powered collections platform focused on optimizing recovery strategies and digital borrower engagement.

- **[Pair Finance](https://www.pairfinance.com/)**  
  Digital debt collection platform using behavioral science and AI to improve recovery rates while maintaining customer relationships.

- **[Receeve](https://receeve.com/)**  
  Collections and recovery platform with automated workflows, payment orchestration, and real-time analytics.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Apache Fineract](https://github.com/apache/fineract)**  
  Apache-licensed core banking platform with comprehensive loan management capabilities including delinquency buckets, charge-off/charge-back transactions, asset sales, and advanced loan search. Version 1.9.0 introduced modular architecture for loan, investor, and provider modules. API-first design with community-maintained UIs. Production-proven at scale for financial institutions globally .

- **[Frappe Lending](https://github.com/Jehad-Alariqi/lending)**  
  Open-source Loan Management System built on ERPNext and Frappe Framework. Covers the entire loan lifecycle from origination to closure, including loan products, collateral management, financial accounting, billing, risk/compliance, and co-lending models. Suitable for NBFCs, microfinance institutions, and lenders seeking a full-featured platform .

- **[LendFlow](https://github.com/Hruthik2311/LendFlow)**  
  Comprehensive loan management and recovery system built with Node.js/Express backend and React 19 frontend. Features multi-role authentication (Admin, Agent, Customer), JWT security, loan applications with approval workflows, EMI payment tracking, recovery agent assignment, real-time notifications, and admin reporting. PostgreSQL database with Sequelize ORM. MIT-style open-source project .

- **[Tira](https://github.com/lgsurith/Tira)**  
  Intelligent AI Collections Agent with self-learning capabilities for debt collection calls. Built with LiveKit, Supabase, and Google Gemini. Features outbound calling, real-time conversation management, multi-language support, financial hardship detection, payment agreement tracking, dispute identification, and FDCPA-compliant conversation flows. Includes LLM-as-a-Judge evaluation framework for automated prompt improvement .

- **[Darj Smart Collection](https://github.com/mym1359/darj-smart-collection)**  
  AI-powered debt recovery system for banking environments based on real-world experience from Maskan Bank (Iran). Analyzes customer repayment behavior using XGBoost and machine learning to predict repayment likelihood and recommend optimal collection strategies (call, warning, legal). Features automated reminders, action logging, branch/user dashboards, and automated letter generation. FastAPI backend with Streamlit frontend .

- **[Recoverflow](https://github.com/JudyaiLab/recoverflow)**  
  Cross-border B2B debt recovery multi-agent system built for regulated high-stakes workflows. Features a 16-reason Voice Escalation Routing Tree, Concierge SLA Matrix with priority tiers (standard, high_24h, critical_immediate), live USDC settlement on Circle ARC blockchain, and a 4-state payment reconciler (PARTIAL, FULL, OVERPAID, UNMATCHED). Designed for Taiwan SME → US buyer scenarios .

- **[N8nDebtCollector](https://github.com/MaDhuManodya/N8nDebtCollector)**  
  Automated debt recovery workflow using n8n, AI (OpenRouter), and Supabase/PostgreSQL. Features AI-powered chatbot for debtor interactions, call automation via Twilio/Plivo, RAG for context-aware responses, and Google Sheets integration for debtor data management. MIT licensed with comprehensive error handling and logging .

- **[Prestamos Gota a Gota](https://github.com/ChrithianC5Develop/prestamos-gota-a-gota)**  
  Professional loan management system with collection and notification features, designed for daily, weekly, biweekly, and monthly loans. Features loan and payment management, collection routes system, multi-channel notifications, JWT authentication, reports/statistics, and multilingual support (Spanish/English). FastAPI backend, MySQL database, Streamlit web frontend, and Kotlin Multiplatform mobile/desktop client. MIT licensed .

- **[Vasool](https://github.com/sriramvarun0636/Vasool)**  
  Revenue-recovery agent for Razorpay with a built-in evaluation harness. Implements a taxonomy-driven approach to payment recovery with safety predicates that prevent automated actions on risk-declined payments. Includes hash-chained receipts for auditability and falsification criteria documented before test runs. Demonstrates the trade-off between recovery rate and deployability in payment recovery systems .

- **[Smart Loan Recovery System](https://github.com/DavieObi/Smart-Loan-Recovery-System)**  
  AI-driven solution that predicts loan default risk using ML, segments borrowers, and assigns dynamic, tailored recovery strategies. Enhances efficiency, optimizes resource allocation, and improves loan recovery rates for effective debt management. 53+ stars on GitHub .

- **[Outstanding Dashboard](https://github.com/adriansanthosh77/outstanding-dashboard)**  
  Open-source payment tracking and analytics platform for GTM operators. Aggregates payment data in a centralized dashboard, categorizes customers by payment behavior (on-time vs. delayed), provides actionable insights to optimize collection strategies, and helps identify high-risk accounts before they become bad debt. HTML-based, free to fork and customize with AI/ML models .

- **[OpenCBS](https://github.com/Axon-System/opencbsys)**  
  Open-source loan tracking software for microfinance institutions, forked from the last open-source version of Octopus Microfinance Suite. Features client management, loan management, savings management, collateral management, loan schedule generation, loan portfolio tracking, custom fields, standard/custom reports, and plugin support. C# based with PostgreSQL backend .

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **Atlas DCA** — AI-driven debt collection platform developed for IIT Madras FedEx Hackathon. Recovery Prediction Engine trained on 800k+ records using XGBoost and Random Forest with 85% ROC score, PostgreSQL with Row-Level Security, and event-driven compliance system .
- **loan-management-system (chandachewe10)** — JavaScript-based loan management software with 106 stars and 82 forks on GitHub .
- **Debt Collective Membership** — Member dues and donations platform, relevant for organizations managing membership-based collections .
- **Debt Collective Disputes API** — Document generation and dispute management service, applicable to collections workflows .

**Frameworks for building custom collections solutions**: Combine **Apache Fineract** or **Frappe Lending** for core loan management, **Tira** or **N8nDebtCollector** for AI-powered borrower engagement, and **Darj Smart Collection** for predictive repayment behavior modeling. For B2B cross-border recovery, **Recoverflow** provides a multi-agent architecture with on-chain settlement. For payment tracking dashboards, **Outstanding Dashboard** offers a lightweight starting point. Note that true enterprise collections management with regulatory compliance reporting remains largely commercial territory; open-source stacks provide loan management, recovery workflows, and AI-powered engagement without the full suite integration of commercial platforms.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Collections management tools must comply with debt collection regulations (FDCPA, GDPR, local consumer protection laws) and financial services compliance requirements.
- Self-hosted open-source solutions require proper infrastructure, security hardening, and ongoing maintenance. AI-powered collection agents must be deployed with appropriate compliance guardrails.
- The open-source ecosystem provides strong loan management and AI-powered recovery capabilities, but full enterprise collections platforms with regulatory reporting and multi-jurisdiction compliance remain primarily commercial offerings.
